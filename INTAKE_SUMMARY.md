# Herban Alchemy - Website Intake Summary
**Project:** Organic skincare / small e-commerce website for Herban Alchemy
**Source:** beauty_website_intake.pdf (Lumen Labs ATL Beauty Products Client Intake)
**Date Retained:** 2026-06-03 (via Grok Build session)

## Brand Overview
- **Full Name:** Herban Alchemy
- **Tagline / Positioning:** Natural skincare. "A bougetto beauty brand"
- **Focus:** All-natural, clean body products with proven effectiveness, specifically formulated "Next for melanated skin".

## Target Audience
- Black women, ages 18-75
- Love to smell good and care for their skin
- Seeking:
  - Preventative skin care and moisturization
  - Correction of dark spots
  - Maintenance of youthful, glowing skin
- Lifestyle: Value clean, effective, fair-trade, purpose-driven products

## Core Products (Current Focus - 2 Main Lines)
Client quote: "I really have 2 main products for now which is the body butter glaze and it comes in a variety of different scents. And a full body fragrance oil which comes in a variety of different scents. I have many other formulations but not ready to add to site just yet And body oil."

- **LUXURY BODY BUTTER GLAZE** (Body Butter Glaze) – All natural, comes in a **variety of different scents**. The hero product shown on white jars with signature logo. Creamy, indulgent texture.
- **FULL BODY FRAGRANCE OIL** (Fragranced Full Body Oils / Body Oil) – Comes in a **variety of different scents**. Lightweight, fragrant body oil.

Many other formulations exist but **not ready** for the site yet. Focus like Crowned Skin: main product(s) with many scent variations.

Key ingredients (Cupuacu, Mango butter, Vitamin E) power the benefits: intense hydration, nourish & soften, healthy radiant glow for melanated skin.

## Brand Values, Story & USPs
- Clean ingredients with **proven effectiveness**
- Tailored specifically for **melanated skin**
- **Fair trade** sourcing — supports the farmers and women who produce the ingredients
- **Charitable impact**: A portion of each sale goes to a charity that supports urban youth in obtaining entrepreneurial skills
- Sustainable / natural / organic focus
- Cruelty-free implied (clean body products)

## Visual Direction, Branding & Layout (Updated Client Feedback)
- **Base colors**: White with black accents (still main).
- **Tone**: NOT so earthy. Needs **colorful photos and content** to give the page a more appealing, vibrant, lively look.
- **Inspiration**: 
  - **Loves the Fenty website layout a ton** – create something **very similar but enhanced just a tad**.
  - Loves the **liveliness and colorfulness and photos like the Fenty site**.
  - Likes Crowned Skin's focused approach (one main product with many variations/scents).
- **Logo**: The exact one on the body butter jars (white jar, black stylized geometric "HERBAN ALCHEMY" with gold "Au 79" badge, "LUXURY BODY GLAZE", "NOURISH • HYDRATE • GLOW 8 OZ").
- **Assets**: Colors, logo, and product visuals from Desktop/herban2.0 folder (vibrant tropical fruit flatlays with mango/pineapple, cupuacu, bright pink/yellow/green/purple backgrounds, creamy textures, indulgent styling).
- **Imagery style**: High-quality, colorful, juicy, vibrant product shots + lifestyle (not earthy tones). Use bright backdrops, fruits, bold graphics like in the provided herban3 image ("RICH. HYDRATED. GLOWING.", colorful "BODY GLAZE" text).
