OUR STORY

Alchemy for your skin.

WHAT'S IN A NAME?

Herban Alchemy

Pronounced HER-bən — it sounds like Urban Alchemy.

The H is silent. We hear it all the time: people say "HER-ban" with a hard H. That's not us. Think urban — city roots, culture, transformation — woven with alchemy: turning pure plants into gold-standard glow for melanated skin.

HER-bən = Urban Alchemy silent H

Herban honors botanicals, heritage, and the hustle of our communities. Alchemy is the craft — fair-trade butters, oils, and raw ingredients transformed into formulas that actually work on deeper skin tones. Together, the name is a promise: rooted in nature, elevated like art, made for us.

Herban Alchemy was born from a love of rich, vibrant botanicals and the belief that melanated skin deserves formulas that truly deliver — never ashy, never watered down, always unforgettable.

The Vision

We create clean, effective body care that celebrates deeper skin tones. No compromises. Just luxurious textures and scents that make you feel powerful and beautiful.

How We Source

Every ingredient is chosen with intention. We work directly with women farmers and producers using fair trade practices — because beauty should uplift the hands that grow it.

A portion of every sale supports programs that teach urban youth entrepreneurial skills.

What we make

  • Luxury Body Butter Glaze — our hero whip, including the limited Atlanta Collection (Sweet Auburn, Peachtree, Lenox).
  • Full Body Fragrance Oil — lightweight, layerable scent stories.
  • Raw White Shea & Raw Mango Butter — 16 oz (1 lb) in signature black bags, portioned as four 4 oz blocks for easy use at home.
