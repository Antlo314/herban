Shipping & Returns

Clear, fair, and designed to make your glow journey effortless.

Shipping Policy

Domestic Shipping (United States)

  • Standard Shipping: 3–7 business days • $8 (Free on orders $65+)
  • Expedited Shipping: 2–3 business days • $15
  • Next Day Delivery: Order by 12pm EST • $25 (available in select areas)

International Shipping

We ship to Canada, UK, EU, and select countries. Rates and delivery times vary by location (typically 7–14 business days).

  • Canada: Starting at $18 USD
  • UK / EU: Starting at $22 USD
  • Rest of World: Starting at $28 USD

Duties and taxes may apply and are the responsibility of the customer.

Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1–2 business days (excluding weekends and holidays). You will receive a shipping confirmation email with tracking once your order has shipped.

Returns & Exchanges

30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

We want you to love your rituals. If you're not completely satisfied, you may return or exchange unopened products within 30 days of delivery.

Return Process

  1. Email hello@herbanalchemy.com with your order number and reason.
  2. We will send you a prepaid return label (US only) or instructions.
  3. Return the product(s) in original packaging.
  4. Once received and inspected, we will issue a refund to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

Exchanges

We’re happy to exchange for a different scent or product of equal value. Just let us know in your return request.

What cannot be returned:
  • Opened or used products (for hygiene reasons)
  • Gift cards
  • Products purchased during final sale events

Refunds

Refunds will be issued to the original form of payment. Please allow 3–5 business days for the refund to appear on your statement (longer for some credit cards or international orders).

If your return is approved, you will receive a confirmation email.
