# Herban Alchemy - Quick Layout & Sitemap Proposal
**Based solely on the intake form (beauty_website_intake.pdf).**  
This is a high-level structure and page-by-page section outline. **No code or assets built yet.** Waiting for your additional information (logos, exact products/pricing/photos, colors, more details) before proceeding to implementation.

## Overall Site Architecture (Sitemap)

1. **Home** (/) - Hero landing, brand story teaser, featured products, values, CTA to shop/quiz
2. **Shop** (/shop) - Full e-commerce grid, filters (by product type, ingredient, scent?), search, categories
3. **Product Detail** (/shop/[slug]) - Individual product pages with description, benefits, ingredients, images, add-to-cart, related products
4. **About** (/about) - Brand story, mission, founder/team (TBD), impact (fair trade + charity)
5. **Ingredients** (/ingredients) - Deep education page on hero ingredients (Cupuacu, Mango Butter, Vitamin E + others), science-backed benefits, "for melanated skin" focus
6. **Blog** (/blog) - Educational articles, how-tos, ingredient spotlights, skin care tips (supports SEO + authority)
7. **Quiz** (/quiz) - Interactive product recommendation quiz (skin type, concerns: dark spots, dryness, glow, etc.)
8. **Contact** (/contact) - Form + strong emphasis on messenger/chat integration

**Footer (global):** Shop links, About, Ingredients, Blog, Contact, Social, Newsletter signup, Charity mention, Legal (shipping, privacy, returns)

**Additional flows:**
- Cart / Checkout (modal or dedicated page)
- Account / Subscriptions (future)
- Before/After Gallery (could be on Home, Shop, or dedicated /results)
